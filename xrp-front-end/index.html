FAQ

Do you ever hold my XRP?

No. XRP Front End is a self-custody interface. Transactions are signed by your wallet and settle directly on the XRP Ledger.

What is a Batch payment?

The XRPL BatchV1_1 amendment lets one transaction carry up to 8 payments, which makes payroll, payouts and splits much faster.

Can I keep business and personal wallets apart?

Yes. Workspaces let you organize multiple XRPL wallets into separate environments in one interface.

Is this affiliated with Ripple?

No. XRP Front End is independent and not affiliated with Ripple Labs.
